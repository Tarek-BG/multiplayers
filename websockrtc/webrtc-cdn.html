📋 How to Connect PC ↔ Smartphone:

  1. On both devices: Open this page
  2. On both devices: Click "Start Camera" (allow permissions)
  3. On both devices: Click "Connect to Network"
  4. Choose a connection method:
    • PeerJS (Recommended): Enter the same room name on both devices and click "Join Room"
    • Manual: Copy your Peer ID from one device and paste it on the other
  5. Optional: Use QR code to easily share room with smartphone
  6. The connection will be established automatically!
⚠️ Important Notes:
  • Both devices must be on the same network or have public IP access
  • Some networks/firewalls may block WebRTC (try mobile hotspot)
  • For best results, use Chrome or Firefox on both devices
